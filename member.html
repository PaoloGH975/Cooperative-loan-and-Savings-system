Cooperative Loan Terms & Undertakings

    1. Membership and Eligibility

    • Membership is subject to the qualifications, requirements, and approval procedures established by the Cooperative and its approved bylaws.
    • Applicants must provide complete, accurate, and truthful information and submit all required documents.
    • Applicants must complete the required Pre-Membership Education Seminar (PMES), when applicable.

    2. Member Responsibilities

    • Members must keep their personal information and contact details accurate and updated.
    • Members must protect their account credentials, passwords, and confidential information.
    • Members must comply with the Cooperative's policies, bylaws, rules, and lawful decisions.

    3. Share Capital and Savings

    • Share Capital represents the member's ownership interest in the Cooperative.
    • Share Capital is not treated as ordinary withdrawable savings and may be subject to applicable restrictions.
    • Members must maintain the required share capital and savings contributions according to Cooperative policies.

    4. Loan Application and Approval

    • All loan applications are subject to evaluation, verification, credit assessment, and approval.
    • Submission of a loan application does not guarantee approval or disbursement.
    • Additional documents or requirements may be requested depending on the loan.
    • Applicant must strictly maintain a minimum of ₱6,000.00 in non-withdrawable Share Capital during the tenure of the loan.
    • Loans are subject to a 1% Cooperative processing fee and credit life insurance deduction upon approval.

    5. Loan Repayment and Delinquency

    • Members must pay their loan installments according to the approved repayment schedule.
    • Payments must be made on or before the applicable due date.
    • Delinquent installments incur a 2% penalty surcharge per month after 30 days past due, subject to applicable policies and laws.
    • Continued failure to pay may result in collection actions and other lawful remedies.

    6. Loan Charges and Deductions

    • Applicable fees and deductions may be deducted from the approved loan proceeds before disbursement.
    • These may include the 1% processing fee and credit life insurance premiums or deductions.
    • The member shall be informed of applicable deductions and net loan proceeds.

    7. Account Status

    • A member's account may be classified as Active, Inactive, Suspended, or Delinquent according to Cooperative policies.
    • Inactive or suspended accounts may have limited access to certain services.

    8. Transparency and Financial Information

    • The Cooperative shall strive to maintain transparency regarding applicable fees, loan terms, account balances, and payment schedules.
    • Members may request access to records or information as permitted by law, bylaws, and Cooperative policies.

    9. Privacy and Personal Information

    • The Cooperative may collect and process personal information for legitimate Cooperative purposes.
    • Personal information shall be handled in accordance with applicable Philippine data privacy laws.
    • Members should report suspected unauthorized access or misuse of their personal information.

    10. Fraud and Unauthorized Transactions

    • Providing false, misleading, altered, or fraudulent information or documents is prohibited.
    • Unauthorized use of another member's account, identity, or credentials is prohibited.
    • Confirmed violations may result in disciplinary measures, recovery of losses, and/or appropriate legal action.

    11. Violations and Disciplinary Action

    • Members who violate Cooperative rules, bylaws, agreements, or applicable laws may be subject to disciplinary action.
    • Actions may include written notice, suspension of privileges, account restrictions, or termination where legally permitted.
    • Members shall be given applicable notice and an opportunity to respond when required.

    12. Legal Action and Collection

    • If a member fails to fulfill a financial obligation, the Cooperative may pursue lawful remedies to recover the outstanding obligation.
    • Remedies may include collection procedures, demand letters, settlement arrangements, or legal proceedings when appropriate.
    • Legal action shall be undertaken in accordance with applicable laws, agreements, and Cooperative bylaws.

    13. Complaints and Appeals

    • Members may submit complaints or concerns through the Cooperative's designated grievance procedure.
    • Complaints shall be handled according to Cooperative policies, bylaws, and applicable laws.

    14. Amendments

    • The Cooperative may update these Terms when necessary to reflect changes in policies, regulations, or applicable laws.
    • Material changes shall be communicated to members through appropriate channels when required.

    15. Governing Law

    These Terms and Agreement shall be interpreted and implemented in accordance with applicable laws of the Republic of the Philippines and the Cooperative's approved bylaws and policies.
